## 🎯 What Is Resonant?

Resonant is a digital audio workstation (DAW) built with Electron and TypeScript, optimized for Windows. It combines traditional music production features with AI-powered generation, making it easy to create original songs, experiment with sounds, and produce professional-quality audio. All processing happens locally on your machine, so your data stays private and you can work offline.

## ✨ Key Features

- **AI Song Generation** – Generate full songs from simple prompts using built-in AI models.
- **Virtual Instruments** – Play a variety of instruments using your keyboard or MIDI controller.
- **Multi-Track Arranger** – Arrange audio and MIDI tracks on a timeline with drag-and-drop ease.
- **Mixer & Effects** – Adjust volume, pan, EQ, reverb, and more with an intuitive mixer.
- **WAV Export** – Export your finished projects as high-quality WAV files.
- **MCP Connectivity** – Connect Codex or Claude through Model Context Protocol for advanced AI assistance.

## 🎹 How to Use Resonant

### Generate a Song
1. Open Resonant and click **New Project**.
2. Select **AI Song Generator** from the toolbar.
3. Enter a description (e.g., "upbeat electronic dance track").
4. Click **Generate** and wait for the AI to create your song.
5. Edit the generated tracks in the arranger.

### Play Instruments
1. Click **Instruments** in the sidebar.
2. Choose a virtual instrument (piano, guitar, drums, synth, etc.).
3. Use your computer keyboard or connect a MIDI controller to play live.
4. Record your performance by clicking the red **Record** button.

### Arrange & Mix
1. Drag audio clips or MIDI patterns onto the timeline.
2. Cut, copy, paste, and loop sections to build your arrangement.
3. Open the **Mixer** to adjust levels, add effects, and pan tracks.
4. Preview your mix with the **Play** button.

### Export Your Work
1. When finished, go to **File > Export**.
2. Choose **WAV** as the format.
3. Select quality settings (sample rate, bit depth).
4. Click **Export** and save your file.

### Connect AI Assistants (MCP)
1. Go to **Settings > MCP**.
2. Enable **Model Context Protocol**.
3. Enter your Codex or Claude API key (if required).
4. Use the AI assistant to help with lyrics, chord progressions, or arrangement ideas.
